How review and progress work

Try recalling the missing words before revealing. Vitanova uses FSRS at 90% desired retention. Again means you forgot; Hard means you recalled with effort. The delay under each rating is calculated for that card. Existing due dates stay unchanged until the next review.

Due + new puts due cards first. Cram does not change your schedule. Skipping changes no study record. Changing choices leaves the active session intact until you start a new one. Undo restores the previous rating’s record. Continue next set keeps the same selection. Learning and relearning cards return as soon as they are due, ahead of unseen cards. When the queue is empty, you can choose to review a learning card up to 20 minutes early. Skipped cards are also left out of later sets. Next lecture follows the same subject’s lecture order, keeps review mode and set size, and clears the topic search. Starting a new session or refreshing begins a new series.

Your flashcard history stays in this browser and is included in Vitanova’s existing backup/import. MCQ bookmarks, mistakes and lecture completion are separate and unchanged. Cards with several blanks for the same cloze reveal them together, including source “one-by-one” notes.

Imported card text is preserved; source content has not undergone a new medical accuracy review. Supplemental source fields are optional and load only when opened.
