How review and progress work

Try recalling the missing words before revealing. Repeat schedules a review in 1 minute; Hard in 6 minutes; Good in 10 minutes; Easy in 2 days. These intervals apply to every new rating; your existing due dates stay unchanged until you rate the card again. These are Vitanova’s simple intervals, not Anki’s scheduling algorithm.

Due + new shows overdue cards first and then unseen cards. Free practice also updates your review schedule. Skipping changes no study record. Changing choices leaves the active session intact until you start a new one. Undo restores the previous rating’s record. Continue next set keeps the same selection and advances to cards not yet shown in this series, even if an earlier card becomes due again. Skipped cards are also left out of later sets. Next lecture follows the same subject’s lecture order, keeps review mode and set size, and clears the topic search. Starting a new session or refreshing begins a new series.

Your flashcard history stays in this browser and is included in Vitanova’s existing backup/import. MCQ bookmarks, mistakes and lecture completion are separate and unchanged. Cards with several blanks for the same cloze reveal them together, including source “one-by-one” notes.

Imported card text is preserved; source content has not undergone a new medical accuracy review. Supplemental source fields are optional and load only when opened.
