PHARMACOLOGY 2

Summary

Trichogenic

Promote growth: prolong anagen, thicker denser shafts (AGA).

Antitrichogenic

Inhibit/remove hair: push follicles to catagen/telogen (hirsutism, hypertrichosis).

Hair cycle Basics

  • Anagen 2–6 years: active growth, new cells.
  • Catagen 2–4 weeks: growth stops, follicle detaches.
  • Telogen 3–5 months: rest, then shedding; prepares the next cycle.
  • Hair follicle = dynamic mini-organ cycling through growth, regression and rest.

Androgenetic alopecia (AGA) Disease

  • Most common alopecia; male- and female-pattern hair loss.
  • Genetic susceptibility + androgens, mainly DHT (testosterone → DHT by 5α-reductase).
  • DHT acts on follicular androgen receptors → miniaturization, shorter anagen, more telogen.
  • Goals: reduce loss, stimulate regrowth, increase density and thickness.
  • Alopecia areata is different: autoimmune patchy loss, treated with immunosuppression.

Minoxidil Trichogenic

Spot it: K⁺ channel opener + vasodilator.
  • Designed as an antihypertensive; K⁺ channel opener (ATP-dependent) → vasodilation → ↑ follicular blood flow.
  • Prolongs anagen; direct proliferative effect on follicle stem cells.
  • Topical solution/foam 2%, 5%; first-line for male and female AGA.
  • Visible benefit takes 3–6 months; loss resumes if stopped.
  • Oral use: monitor BP, heart rate, edema; may cause hypertrichosis.

Finasteride Trichogenic

  • 5α-reductase inhibitor → blocks testosterone → DHT (the hormone that shrinks follicles).
  • Oral tablet (male AGA); improvement 3–6 months, full assessment ~12 months.
  • Adverse: ↓ libido, erectile dysfunction, ejaculatory disorders, breast tenderness/enlargement, mood changes/depression.
  • Contraindicated in pregnancy: DHT inhibition impairs male fetal external genitalia.

PRP and botanicals Adjuncts

  • PRP: injected concentrated platelets from the patient’s own blood release growth factors (e.g. VEGF) → follicle regeneration, prolonged anagen.
  • Caffeine: counteracts DHT effects.
  • Rosemary oil: in some studies as effective as 2% minoxidil with less scalp itching.

Eflornithine Antitrichogenic

Spot it: Ornithine → (ODC ✕) → putrescine → spermidine → spermine.
  • Irreversible inhibitor of ornithine decarboxylase → ↓ polyamines needed for matrix cell division and hair growth.
  • Topical; facial hirsutism: effective in ~30% of women at 6 months; noticeable at 6–8 weeks.
  • Does not treat the underlying hyperandrogenism.
  • Adverse: local skin reactions.

Spironolactone Anti-androgen

  • Blocks androgen effects on the follicle.
  • Used mainly for female hirsutism and pattern hair loss; not routinely recommended for male AGA.
  • Adverse: menstrual irregularity, breast tenderness, dizziness/hypotension, hyperkalemia, ↑ urination.

High-yield

  • Minoxidil = K⁺ channel opener; Finasteride = 5α-reductase inhibitor; Eflornithine = ODC inhibitor.
  • Finasteride never in pregnancy; stopping minoxidil/finasteride reverses benefit.
